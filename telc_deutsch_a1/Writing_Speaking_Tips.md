**Writing (Schreiben)**



There are two tasks, and both are predictable.



Task 1 — filling out a form. You read a short text about a person and transfer the info into a form. The whole game here is knowing the form vocabulary cold, because if you don't recognize the field label you can't fill it. Drill these until they're automatic: Vorname, Familienname/Nachname, Geburtsdatum, Geburtsort, Wohnort, Straße/Hausnummer, Postleitzahl (PLZ), Land, Staatsangehörigkeit, Familienstand (ledig/verheiratet), Beruf, Telefonnummer, Unterschrift. Also practice writing dates and numbers correctly, since those trip people up.



Task 2 — a short message (Kurzmitteilung), usually an email or note to a friend. You get three bullet points you must address. Scoring rewards a clear structure, so memorize a skeleton and just slot content in:



Greeting: Liebe Anna, / Hallo Tom,

Opening line: vielen Dank für deine E-Mail. or wie geht es dir?

One sentence per bullet point (that's the core — make sure all three appear)

Closing: Viele Grüße / Liebe Grüße + your name



Common themes are inviting someone, canceling or rescheduling, apologizing, thanking, asking for something. Prepare a few reusable sentences for each (e.g. Leider kann ich nicht kommen, weil… / Kannst du mir bitte helfen? / Wollen wir uns am Samstag treffen?). Write 5–6 practice messages and get them checked — repetition on the template matters more than range.







**Speaking (Sprechen) — done in a small group with other candidates.**



Part 1 — introduce yourself. Fixed categories: name, age, country, where you live, languages, job, hobby. Fully memorize a 6–8 sentence intro. You'll also draw two cards and spell a word and say a number, so practice the Alphabet (spelling your name aloud) and saying numbers.



Part 2 — ask and answer questions on a theme. You get a card with a topic word (e.g. Essen, Familie, Beruf, Einkaufen, Tagesablauf, Wohnung) and must form a question with it, then answer your partner's. The skill being tested is W-questions, so drill the pattern: Wo…? Was…? Wann…? Wie viel…? Wie oft…? Practice generating a quick question from any noun.



Part 3 — make and respond to requests. You get picture cards (an object or action) and must ask someone to do something, then respond to theirs. Memorize the polite request forms: Können Sie bitte…? / Kannst du mir bitte… geben? and the responses: Ja, gerne. / Ja, natürlich. / Einen Moment, bitte.
